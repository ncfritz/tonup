## Description

Ton Up is a darts scorer, trainer and rule book for your iPhone, built to stay out of the way at the oche.

SCORE BY TAPPING THE BOARD
Tap where each dart landed, or hold and drag for a precise spot. Ton Up works out the score, shows your checkout, and keeps where every dart went, so replays and heatmaps work from your first leg. Prefer numbers? Use the keypad.

PLAY
• 501, 301 and 701 with double, master or straight out, legs and sets
• Cricket and Cut-Throat Cricket, Around the Clock, Shanghai and Killer
• Pass the phone round, or play a bot at five levels
• A caller reads out each visit

PRACTISE
• Bob's 27, Count Up and the Around the Clock drill
• Personal bests, trends and a suggested drill from your recent games

LEARN
• Rules for 26 darts games, every checkout from 170, board setup and a glossary

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone; export a backup whenever you like.

TON UP PRO, ONE PURCHASE
• 21 more games: cricket variants, 201 double in double out, Gotcha, Fifty-One by Fives, Nine Lives, Hare and Hounds, Halve-It, Bermuda Triangle, Blind Killer, Scam, Baseball, Golf, Football, Tic-Tac-Toe and more
• The full practice library: 100 darts at T20, Doubles Lock, Catch 40, 121, Random Checkout, the JDC Challenge and more, a monthly skill assessment with a daily plan, a checkout trainer and pressure mode
• Deeper analysis: grouping and drift, where to aim for your grouping, weekly trends, and a ghost bot that throws like you
• Four more themes: Chalkboard, Arcade, Heritage and Training
• Camera scoring, coming in a future update and included in Pro
Pay once; Pro is shared with your Family Sharing group.
