# Naming

- Decided: 2026-11-02. The app is **Ton Up**; the domain **tonup.app** is registered.
- A "ton" is a visit of 100 or more, so the name means scoring better. It's short, easy to say at the board, and "Ton Up Pro" reads naturally for the upgrade.
- Constraint: a `.app` domain. Availability was checked against the `.app` registry's RDAP service on 2026-11-02.

| Candidate | `.app` status on 2026-11-02 |
| --- | --- |
| tonup, dartling, trebletwenty, dartsmith, dartwise, ochelab, ocheclub | Free (tonup since registered) |
| oche, theoche, doubletop, doubleout, outshot, ninedarter, throwline, tungsten, treblemaker, barrelhouse, ton80, chalkup, arrowsmith | Taken |

Notes on the runners-up: "Oche" is a darts-bar chain with outlets in several countries ([Oche franchise](https://www.franchising.com/oche/)), and a training app called DartsLab already exists, so the Oche names were weaker. "Ton-up" is also motorcycle slang.
