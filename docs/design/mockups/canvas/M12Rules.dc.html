Cricket

2–4 players · 15–25 min · Targets 15–20 and bull

Close each number by hitting it three times, then score on it until your opponent closes it too. Close everything with the most points to win.

Single = 1 mark
Double = 2
Treble = closed
Scoring. Extra marks on a number you've closed add its value, as long as an opponent hasn't closed it.
Bull. Outer bull is 1 mark, inner bull 2.
Winning. Close all seven with points equal to or ahead of everyone else.
